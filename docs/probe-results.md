# Probe results: LLMRouterBench, Qwen3.5-9B activations

Generated by `training/train_probe.py` on 2026-11-12: 4157 prompts (3325 train / 832 held-out test, stratified by dataset), encoder `Qwen3.5-9B-Q8_0.gguf`, prompts truncated to 1024 tokens, thinking=True.

## Tiers

The 13 benchmark models are ranked by overall success on all 12,446 LLMRouterBench prompts and split into thirds. A tier's label on a prompt is the fraction of its models that answered it (score ≥ 0.5); its token target is their mean output tokens.

| Tier | Models (overall success) |
|---|---|
| small | intern-s1 (0.38), claude-sonnet-4 (0.39), deepseek-v3-0324 (0.42), gemini-2.5-flash (0.44), deepseek-v3.1-terminus (0.45) |
| medium | glm-4.6 (0.45), kimi-k2-0905 (0.45), deepseek-r1-0528 (0.48), gpt-5-chat (0.48) |
| large | qwen3-235b-a22b-2507 (0.54), qwen3-235b-a22b-thinking-2507 (0.54), gpt-5 (0.59), gemini-2.5-pro (0.60) |
| xlarge | none; head extrapolated as large + (large − medium) in log-odds |

**Metrics.** *AUROC*: tier prediction against each member model's success, averaged over tiers. *Within-dataset AUROC*: the same inside each benchmark, averaged; how well difficulty is ranked beyond knowing the benchmark. *Tokens ρ*: Spearman correlation of predicted and actual mean output tokens.

### Routing between small / medium / large
